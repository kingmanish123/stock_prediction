# Capital Deployment Plan

Philosophy: **crawl, walk, run.** Don't deploy capital before earning the right to.

---

## Phase 0 — Preparation (Month 1–2)
**Capital deployed**: ₹0
**Activity**: Reading, coding, backtesting
**Exit criteria**: Book 1 complete + first working backtest + paper trading system ready

---

## Phase 1 — Paper Trading (Month 3–5)
**Capital deployed**: ₹0 (simulated only)
**Activity**: Live market data, simulated orders, daily tracking
**Exit criteria**:
- 90 days of paper trades logged
- Paper Sharpe > 0.8 after realistic costs
- Psychological check: did I break any rules mentally during drawdowns?
- No fatal system bugs

---

## Phase 2 — Live Small (Month 6–9)
**Capital deployed**: ₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000 (losable without stress)
**Activity**: Live trading, small size, same strategy as paper
**Success criteria**:
- Disciplined execution (no overrides)
- Live PnL within -20%/+20% of paper (proves mechanics work)
- All rules from [04-risk-rules.md](04-risk-rules.md) followed

**Expected outcome**: Small net loss or break-even is NORMAL. This is tuition for real-market frictions (slippage, partial fills, psychology).

---

## Phase 3 — Scale Validation (Month 10–15)
**Capital deployed**: ₹2–3L
**Activity**: Increase position size only if Phase 2 was disciplined (not just profitable)
**Success criteria**:
- Positive Sharpe > 0.8 over Phase 2 + 3 combined
- Max drawdown <20%
- Still following all rules
- Still doing monthly review + journal

---

## Phase 4 — Meaningful Scale (Year 2+)
**Capital deployed**: ₹5L+ (only if Phase 2 + 3 validated)
**Activity**: Increase to meaningful size, potentially add second strategy
**Success criteria**:
- Year-1 outcome documented honestly
- Taxes paid properly
- Spouse/family aware
- Still running systematic, still journaling

## Expected PnL scenarios (realistic, not hyped)

### Scenario A — Median (most likely, ~40% probability)
- Year 1: -5% to +5% (learning, break-even-ish)
- Year 2: +10% to +20%
- Year 3: +15% to +25%
- **Year 3 capital at ₹5L start**: ₹6.5L – ₹7.5L

### Scenario B — Good (~25% probability)
- Year 1: +10% to +15%
- Year 2: +20% to +25%
- Year 3: +20% to +25%
- **Year 3 capital at ₹5L start**: ₹8.5L – ₹9.5L

### Scenario C — Bad (~25% probability)
- Year 1: -10% to -20%
- Decision point: continue (if disciplined) or stop (if strategy broken)
- If continue: Year 2/3 might recover to break-even

### Scenario D — Catastrophic (~10% probability)
- Rule breakage → big loss (-30%+)
- Forced exit from trading

## How to think about "returns"

**Do NOT compare to**:
- Index fund returns (different risk profile, active effort counts)
- Social media "my 200% year" posts (survivorship bias + lies)
- Backtest returns (always optimistic)

**Do compare to**:
- Nifty50 index returns (benchmark)
- Your own past year's performance (self-improvement)
- Risk-adjusted metrics (Sharpe, Calmar)

## Capital sources (honest categorization)

- **Can risk**: Savings beyond emergency fund, after EMIs, after retirement contributions
- **Cannot risk**: Emergency fund (6 months expenses), retirement savings, child education fund, house down payment
- **Definitely cannot risk**: Borrowed money, margin money, family savings

Stay strictly in "can risk" bucket.

## Tax planning

- **Short-term capital gains (STCG)**: <12 months holding = 20% tax (India, post-2024 changes)
- **Long-term capital gains (LTCG)**: >12 months = 12.5% above ₹1.25L/year
- **Intraday/F&O profits**: treated as business income (slab rate)
- **Maintain records**: every trade, fees, broker statements
- **Advance tax**: if trading income >₹10K/quarter, pay advance tax
- **Audit requirement**: if turnover >₹10 crore (unlikely for retail)

**Recommendation**: Use a CA familiar with trading income from Year 2.
